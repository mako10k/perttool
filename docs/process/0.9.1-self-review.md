## 1. Version selection

The accepted change corrects how one existing read-only command selects its
declared source. It does not add or remove a command, option, result, schema,
export, grammar construct, authority rule, or persistence effect. Suffix-free
`0.9.1` is therefore the accurate compatible beta patch after `0.9.0`.

Leaving the change unreleased would strand the corrected installed workflow,
while `0.10.0` would imply a new additive or breaking public boundary that is
not present.
